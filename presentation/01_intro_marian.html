What is RLGym?

RLGym turns the game into a standard RL problem.

It wraps Rocket League in the same reset() and step(action) loop every RL algorithm expects. Observations go in, an action comes out, a reward comes back. Exactly like CartPole, except the world is a car soccer arena.

Session 2 · the loop

Every tick runs the same loop, state, action, reward.

STATE s
What the bot sees (ball, its car, the rival).
ACTION a
What it does (throttle, steer, jump, boost).
REWARD r
A score (+1 a goal, −1 conceded).
POLICY π
Picks the action (this is what we train).
VALUE V
Guesses how good this moment is (the critic).
RETURN
The goal: more reward over time (win more, concede less).
Session 9 · how it picks

Same spot, different move every time.

DETERMINISTICsame state, same move, alwaysnot used for training
STOCHASTICsame state, weighted random movewhat we train with
Train. Sample from a distribution over 90 moves.
Test. Switch to deterministic, take the top move, for a clean read.
Bonus. Randomness already is exploring. No separate explore step needed.

Session 2 · reward shaping

You can't just say "win." You reward it, step by step.

DENSEtiny reward, every tickguides behavior
SPARSErare, worth 12x morewhat actually matters
Dense. Move to ball, face ball, touch ball. Small numbers, constant signal.
Sparse. Goal +1, concede -1, shot +0.4, save +0.4, weight 12.0 overall.
Reward hacking. Too much weight on slow pushing taught slow pushing. Cut 0.8 to 0.5, it started shooting.
Session 9 · self play

It learns by playing itself.

Setup. Every few million steps, freeze a copy of the bot. That's the new opponent.
Early on. A clumsy opponent, because it's also clumsy.
Later. A tough opponent, itself, leveled up.
No dial. Difficulty scales on its own, no one touches a setting.
It is zero sum. Score a goal and the opponent's reward drops by the same amount. Total reward nets to about 0, so we track win rate, not reward, to see real progress.

One algorithm decision

We skipped DQN, used PPO.

DQNlearns from old, replayed gamesnot used
PPOlearns fresh, every batchwhat we use
Why not DQN. Replay buffer goes stale fast when the opponent keeps changing.
Why PPO. Caps how far it moves each update. Built for a moving target.
